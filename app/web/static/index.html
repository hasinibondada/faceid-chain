Face Identification

Detect & encode the face from your input image with OpenCV DNN. Produces a compact face fingerprint.

Web / Social Search

A genuine Google search runs, candidate posts are downloaded, and each one is face-matched against the input to find a real matching post.

3

Blockchain Verification

The matched post's fingerprint is mined onto a tamper-evident local chain, then re-verified from the chain.
